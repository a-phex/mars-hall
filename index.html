About Ash Marshall

I make films that capture how something actually felt: the buzz of an awards night, the character of a location, the moment a customer starts talking like they mean it.

I run projects from the first conversation to the final delivery, so the idea, the shoot, the edit, colour and sound all come from one place. Whatever the brief or the budget, I'll find a way to get it made, and made well.

What clients come to me for:

Putting the feeling of an event or a place on screen

Making interviewees and customers comfortable on camera

Brand and customer stories that make complex ideas simple

One person owning it end to end, from idea to final cut

Most people aren't used to being filmed. A lot of the work happens before the camera rolls: a chat, a coffee, a bit of trust, so that when we start recording, people sound like themselves.

On the day, I'm watching the room as much as the shot: the light, the noise, the reactions. That's what lets me bring the atmosphere home, so someone who wasn't there still gets it.

If you can feel it in the room, I can get it on screen.
